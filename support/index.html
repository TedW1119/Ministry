Ministry Support

Church scheduling companion for iPhone and Android

Ministry helps church volunteers view upcoming schedules, manage personal duties, receive reminders, and sync assignments to a calendar.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add family or team members?
Go to Settings and use the Members section. Enter each name as it appears on the church schedule. You can follow multiple members on one device.
Why don't I see any schedules?
Confirm that at least one saved member matches a name on the church schedule, then pull down on the Home screen to refresh. Check your internet connection if the schedule still does not appear.
How do notifications work?
Ministry sends server-based push reminders for upcoming assignments and personal duties. In Settings, you can choose reminder timings for each role. Notifications must be allowed in your device settings.
How do I sync assignments to a calendar?
Open Settings and choose Calendar Sync. On iPhone, choose Apple Calendar or Google Calendar. On Android, connect Google Calendar. Ministry creates a separate calendar named Ministry and updates it when you tap Sync Now.
Why did Google ask for calendar permission?
Ministry needs permission to create and manage the dedicated Ministry calendar. It also reads calendar-list metadata to find that calendar again across devices. It does not read events from unrelated calendars.
How do I create a personal duty?
Tap the add button on the Home screen, enter the duty details and reminder timings, then save.
How do I delete my data?
Removing the App deletes local settings. You can delete the Ministry calendar in your calendar app and revoke Google access from your Google Account. For deletion of backend information associated with your installation, email ministry.shigong@gmail.com.
What information does Ministry process?
Ministry processes followed member names, reminder preferences, an installation identifier, push-delivery information, personal duties, and limited diagnostics. Google Calendar access is optional. See the Privacy Policy for complete details.

Report a problem

Use Report a Bug in the App's Settings tab, or email ministry.shigong@gmail.com. Include what you expected, what happened, and your phone platform.
